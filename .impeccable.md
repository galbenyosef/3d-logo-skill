## Design Context

### Users
Frontend devs who use a coding agent (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex…). They land from a Reddit/X post or the GitHub repo, want to see their own logo spin within seconds, then copy one install command. Most visits are one short look, often on a phone.

### Brand Personality
Weightless, precise, a little dreamy. The page is a dusk sky the coin floats in. Copy is minimal and direct: the demo does the talking.

### Aesthetic Direction
STRATOS: a dusk sky (#14163A → #3B3F8F → #C98FB5 → #FFC9A3) with Michroma display caps and JetBrains Mono for everything else. Only a hint of Wipeout survives (the font and the `//` idiom). Everything in the sky is crisp: pin-sharp stars, a hard-edged sun disc, flat cloud banks with lit top edges. Dark theme, because the sky is the product shot.

Anti-references: blurry radial-gradient "clouds", a literal Wipeout re-skin, glassmorphism on every surface, generic SaaS hero templates, neon-on-black.

### Design Principles
1. The coin is the hero. Every other element frames it or gets out of its way.
2. Crisp over soft. No `filter: blur` on decoration; softness comes from colour, not blur.
3. Less text. If a label repeats what the UI already shows, cut it.
4. Screen 1 fits in 100svh at 375×667 and every control works there without scrolling.
5. Type budget: 2 families, 3 sizes, weights 400/500 only.
